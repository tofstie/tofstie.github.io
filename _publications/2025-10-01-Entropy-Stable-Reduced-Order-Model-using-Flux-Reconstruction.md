This paper represents my work done for the completion of my master’s degree. In it, I begin by reviewing existing literature on 
the discontinuous Galerkin (DG) method and entropy stable representation and requirements for entropy stability. Next, the literature
is reviewed for constructing projection based reduced order models (ROM). Next, work is presented to develop an entropy stable
reduced order model (ESROM) that works with flux reconstruction. A relaxation method is then developed for the ESROM. Next, a 
hyper reduction scheme was developed to preserve the entropy stability of the ESROM while reducing the number of floating point
operations (FLOPS) needed to get an approximate solution. Numerical results are then shown for the Euler equations in multiple dimensions and
with different boundary conditions.
