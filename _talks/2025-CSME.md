In this presentation, I presented the entropy stable reduced order model (ESROM) developed for the discontinuous Galerkin method.
The ESROM also works with flux reconstruction methods. This is then further expanded upon by introducing hyper reduction that ensures that the summation-by-parts properties of entropy stability
methods is preserved. Selected test cases using the Euler equations are used in multiple dimensions and boundary conditions
to show that the integrated convective entropy is stable.
