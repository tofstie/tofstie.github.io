In this conference, I presented the entropy stable reduced order model (ESROM) developed for the DG method. I presented results
on a 1D shock test case and 2D test case and compared the results against a standard projection based reduced order model.

[Link to Abstract](https://meetings.siam.org/sess/dsp_talk.cfm?p=143479)
